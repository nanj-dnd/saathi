Uber (cabs, autos, bikes). Trust the screen over these hints.
- "Where to?" box at the top/middle of the home screen: tap it and type or pick the destination.
- Pickup is usually the current location; confirm it matches where the senior is.
- Choose a ride type (Auto, Uber Go, etc.); the price shows next to each. Ask if they want the cheapest.
- "Choose"/"Confirm" button at the bottom books it. Cash or UPI can be chosen before confirming.
- After booking, the screen shows driver name, car number and OTP for the ride: the ride OTP is told to the driver in person, which is safe. Never share it on a phone call.
