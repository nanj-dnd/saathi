Zomato (food delivery). Trust the screen over these hints.
- Search for a dish or restaurant with the search bar near the top.
- Inside a restaurant, dishes have an "ADD" button; some ask to choose size or extras in a sheet that slides up, with an "Add item" button at its bottom.
- A bar at the bottom ("View cart") leads to checkout. Check the address. Payment is for the senior alone.
- Many popups offer offers or Zomato Gold; they can be closed with an X or by tapping outside.
