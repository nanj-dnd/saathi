UMANG (many government services in one app: EPFO/PF, pension, gas booking, Jeevan Pramaan, Aadhaar).
- Use the search bar at the top to find a service, e.g. "EPFO", "pension", "Jeevan Pramaan", "gas".
- Login uses mobile number + OTP or MPIN. The senior enters OTP/MPIN alone.
- Services open their own screens with forms; read field labels to the senior one at a time.
