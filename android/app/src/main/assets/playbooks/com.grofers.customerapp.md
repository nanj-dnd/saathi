Blinkit (groceries in ~10 minutes). Layouts change often; trust the screen over these hints.
- Search: the search bar near the top ("Search ..."). Tap it, then tap the microphone in it if typing is hard.
- Adding: each product card has a small green-outlined "ADD" button. After adding it becomes "- 1 +".
- Cart: a green bar at the bottom ("View cart") appears once something is added.
- Checkout: check the delivery address shown on the cart page before paying. Payment opens a UPI/card screen: the senior does that part alone.
- Common confusion: product size/pack (500 ml vs 1 L). Ask which they want if unclear.
