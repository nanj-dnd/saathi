Web browser (often used for government portals: pension, Jeevan Pramaan, passport, income tax, ration card, electricity board sites).
- Government sites usually end in .gov.in or .nic.in. Warn if a "government" page is on another domain.
- To open a site, tap the address bar at the top and type the address, or use the search.
- Pages are often small: tell the senior they can spread two fingers on the screen to zoom.
- Captchas are for the senior to type; read the instructions slowly.
