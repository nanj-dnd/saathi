Google Play Store. To get an app: the search bar is at the top ("Search apps & games"); after the senior types the app's name and picks it from the list, its page has a green "Install" button. Free apps say "Install"; an app showing a price (₹) costs money, so tell the senior it is paid and let them decide, never point at the price. While downloading the page shows "Cancel" and a progress bar; when finished, a green "Open" button appears. Check the developer name under the app title matches the real company (fake look-alike apps exist), and warn if an app is a remote-control app (AnyDesk, TeamViewer, QuickSupport) someone asked them to install.
